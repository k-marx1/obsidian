# Адиабатный
$$
k=\dfrac{c_{p}}{c_{v}}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
Tv^{k-1}&=const \\
pv^k&=const \\
Tp^{(1-k)/k}&=const
\end{align}
$$
$$
A=c_{v}\left( T_{1}-T_{2} \right) = \dfrac{1}{k-1}\left( p_{1}v_{1} - p_{2}v_{2} \right)=\dfrac{R}{k-1}\left( T_{1}-T_{2} \right)
$$
# Политропный
$$
c=c_{v}\cdot \dfrac{{n-k}}{n-1}
$$
$$
\dfrac{{c-c_{p}}}{c-c_{v}}=n
$$
$$
n=\dfrac{\dfrac{\Delta u}{q}\cdot k-1}{\dfrac{\Delta u}{q}-1}\
$$

# Изохорный
$$
n=\infty
$$$$
q=\Delta u=c_{v}(T_{2}-T_1)
$$
$$
\Delta S=c_{v}\ln \dfrac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}
$$

# Изотермический
$$
n=1
$$
$$
q=A=RT \ln \dfrac{V_{2}}{V_{1}}=RT \ln \dfrac{p_{1}}{p_{2}}=p_{1}v_{1}\ln \dfrac{p_{1}}{p_{2}}=p_{2}v_{2}\ln \dfrac{v_{2}}{v_{1}}
$$
$$
\Delta S=R\ln \dfrac{v_{2}}{v_{1}}
$$
# Изобарный

$$
n=0
$$
$$
dq=di-c_{p}dT
$$
$$
A=p\left( v_{2}-v_{1} \right) =R\left( T_{2}-T_{1} \right) 
$$
$$
dS=\dfrac{dq}{T}
$$


| Процесс        | $n$       | $c_{n}$  |
| -------------- | --------- | -------- |
| Изобарный      | $0$       | $c_{p}$  |
| Изохорный      | $+\infty$ | $c_{v}$  |
| Изотермический | $1$       | $\infty$ |
| Адиабатный     | $k$       | $0$      |
